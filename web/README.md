# G-niuszok: project showcase site

A one-page, Apple-style showcase for team **G-niuszok** at the **V. Junior Engineers Camp – HARMAN Hackathon** (Székesfehérvár, 13–14 Nov 2026).
It doubles as your **8-minute pitch deck** and works **offline**: the fonts and the 3D library are bundled.

The page presents **ChangAR by G-niuszok**: a smart mirror that measures you with a camera and a distance sensor and shows the chosen clothes on you live.

---

## 1. Run it

| You have | Do this |
|---|---|
| Windows | Double-click **`start.bat`**. It opens `http://localhost:8080`. |
| Mac / Linux | In this folder run `python3 -m http.server 8080`, then open `http://localhost:8080`. |
| VS Code | Install the **Live Server** extension, right-click `index.html` and choose **Open with Live Server**. |

> Don't open `index.html` by double-clicking it. Browsers block 3D files loaded straight from disk, and the page will show a warning if you do.

## 2. Present it (the jury gives you 8 minutes)

Open the site and press **P**. Every section becomes a slide, and each Design chapter is a slide of its own.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **→ / ↓ / Space / PageDown** | Next slide (most presentation clickers send these) |
| **← / ↑ / PageUp** | Previous slide |
| **F** | Full screen |
| **T** | Show/hide the 8:00 countdown (it turns orange in the last minute) |
| **R** | Reset the timer to 8:00 |
| **B** or **.** | Black screen, press again to come back |
| **Esc** | Leave presentation mode |

## 3. Make it yours

Search `index.html` for **✏️** to find every place you need to edit. The page follows the organisers' slide template (*Csapat neve_Hackathon 2025.pptx*), in this order:

| Template slide | Section on the page |
|---|---|
| Title: team, product, year | Hero (`#top`) |
| Idea, in keywords | **Az ötlet** (`#overview`) |
| Product 1: features, benefits, prototype | **Hogyan működik?** (`#how`), the 3D story (`#design`) |
| Product 2: uses, further development | **Kinek segít?** (`#impact`), **Mi jön még 5 év alatt?** (`#roadmap`) |
| Innovation and future-proofing: why it matters in 30 years | **Miért lesz fontos 30 év múlva is?** (`#future`) |
| Questions | **Kérdések?** (`#questions`) |
| Goodbye | Closing (`#thanks`) |

Presentation mode (press **P**) has 13 stops (each 3D story step is one) and an 8-minute timer (**T**). The interactive 3D explorer (`#explore`) is on the page only: presentation mode hides it, along with the "Nézd meg 3D-ben" buttons that point to it.

Quick wins:
- **Project name**: *ChangAR*, in the `<title>` and the nav brand. The hero and the closing slide show *ChangAR by G-niuszok*, with both names at the same size (`.lockup__…` classes in `css/style.css`). The team name is also in the team section, the footer and the page description.
- **Colours**: change `--accent-1` … `--accent-4` at the top of `css/style.css`. The whole site follows.
- **Stats**: the big numbers in the highlights grid count up from 0. Set the target in `data-count="…"`.
- **Delete a section**: remove the whole `<section>`. Presentation mode adapts automatically.

## 4. Your 3D model

The site uses Google's [`<model-viewer>`](https://modelviewer.dev), which needs a **`.glb`** file (binary glTF).

**The current model** (`assets/models/changar.glb`) is generated by **`tools/changar-model.html`** with three.js, following photos of the real build: a portrait 24" monitor on a stand, the team's 3D-printed holder hooked over its top edge (`tools/models/stand.3mf`), the 7" touchscreen standing on it in landscape with the Raspberry Pi 4 (heatsink, no fan) on its back, the HC-SR04 on top of the display and the Pi camera in its clip-on mount at the right (`tools/models/camera-mount.3mf`), plus the exploded-view animation. To change it, replace the 3MF files or edit the positions at the top of the script, open `http://localhost:8080/tools/changar-model.html` (it loads three.js from a CDN, so it needs internet), download `changar.glb` and replace the one in `assets/models/`. The page also prints the hotspot positions to paste into `index.html`. The 3MF files are read in their own object coordinates (millimetres); the slicer's build-plate position is ignored. The holder's material is called `Bracket`, which the colour swatches recolour.

1. **Export to `.glb`**
   - **Blender**: *File → Export → glTF 2.0*, Format **glTF Binary (.glb)**, +Y Up.
   - **Onshape / Tinkercad**: they can export glTF or GLB directly.
   - **Fusion 360 / SolidWorks / anything else**: export STL, OBJ or FBX, import it into Blender, then export a `.glb`.
   - STL files have no colours. In Blender, give each part a material with a base colour, metallic and roughness before exporting.
   - Leave **Draco compression off**. Decoding it needs an internet connection.
   - Aim for under 15 MB. Don't use 8K textures.
2. **Swap it in**: put the file in `assets/models/` and change `src="assets/models/changar.glb"` on the three `<model-viewer>` tags (hero, design story, explorer). You can also just name your file `changar.glb`.
3. **Hotspots**: open **`http://localhost:8080/?edit`** and click your model in the explorer. The `data-position` and `data-normal` values are copied to your clipboard. Paste them onto a `<button class="hotspot">`.
4. **Camera angles for the design story**: in `?edit` mode, drag the explorer model to a nice angle and read the values in the panel. Copy the two angles into a step's `data-orbit`, and keep the radius as a percentage (`100%` fills the frame).
5. **Exploded view (optional, very effective)**: in Blender, keyframe each part's location: assembled on frame 1, pulled apart on frame ~24. Export with *Animation* ticked. The design story scrubs it with scroll (`data-explode="1"` on a step), and the **Assembled / Exploded** toggle plays it. Without an animation the toggle hides itself.
6. **Finish swatches**: they recolour the material named in `data-material` (now `Bracket`, the 3D-printed holder). Rename that to your model's main material, or the swatches hide themselves.
7. **AR**: on phones a **View in your space** button appears automatically.

## 6. Put it on nethely.hu

1. **Get the hosting.** Register at [nethely.hu](https://www.nethely.hu/ingyenes-tarhely), activate the free hosting (*Ingyenes tárhely*) and pick a free address, for example `g-niuszok.nhely.hu`.
2. **Upload with FTP.** In the customer portal (*Ügyfélszolgálat*), create an FTP/SFTP user. Connect with WinSCP or FileZilla; nethely's step-by-step guides are under [Tudásbázis → FTP és SFTP](https://www.nethely.hu/tudasbazis/ftp).
   - Upload the **contents** of this folder: `index.html`, `css/`, `js/`, `assets/` and `vendor/`.
   - They go into the folder your web address (*webcím*) points to, so that `index.html` sits at the top of it.
   - `start.bat` and `README.md` are only for you; you don't need to upload them.
   - The browser-based *Fájlkezelő* works too, but FTP is easier for whole folders.
3. **Remove the host's placeholder.** If that folder already has an `index.php` or `index.html` from nethely, delete it. The server might otherwise show that page instead of yours.
4. **Check it.** Open `http://<your-address>.nhely.hu/`. If the 3D model is missing, press **F12 → Console** and look for red *404* errors. The usual causes are a folder that didn't upload or a file name with the wrong capitals. The server is case-sensitive: `ChangAR.glb` is not `changar.glb`.

Good to know about the free plan:

- **It's `http://` only.** nethely can't issue certificates for its free endings (`.nhely.hu`, `.webtelek.hu` …). Everything on the page still works, with two side effects:
  - Chrome 154 and later shows a "not secure" warning page the first time someone opens an http-only site. Visitors have to click through it once.
  - The **View in your space** AR button may not appear on every phone, because browser AR (WebXR) needs HTTPS.
- **256 MB of space** is plenty: the site is about 3 MB.
- **Present from the local copy.** Run `start.bat` on the presenting laptop. It's faster and doesn't depend on the venue Wi-Fi. Use the nethely link for sharing.

Need an `https://` link without the warning, for example for a QR code the jury scans? [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) gives you one for free: drag this folder in. You can use it alongside nethely.
